# Selected Google reviews

`google-reviews.json` is the shared, manually maintained source for the homepage, reviews page, prerendered HTML and public testimonials API. It was checked against the Pahwajee Google Maps listing on 11 October 2026 (14 reviews visible).

Keep reviewer names, ratings and wording as published. Mark shortened quotations as excerpts. Source URLs open the business reviews listing; Google review identifiers are kept for provenance. Do not invent locations, photos or reviews. Recheck the listing before updates and remove reviews no longer published there.

The public endpoint deliberately does not publish legacy database testimonials or admin-created entries automatically. Existing database data is retained, but the old placeholder seed is removed. Add future source-checked Google reviews to this file and redeploy. This is a curated snapshot, not a live Google feed.
